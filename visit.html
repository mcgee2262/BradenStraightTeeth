FAQ

Are your teeth real fossils?

Yes. Every tooth is a genuine fossil except the clearly labeled megalodon replica.

Will you identify a tooth I found?

Always, and always free. Bring it in or send a photo through the contact form.

Do you ship?

Teeth are padded, boxed and shipped coast to coast. Orders over $75 ship free.

Where’s the best place to hunt nearby?

Anywhere along Oak Island’s shoreline at low tide, especially after a storm. Check out our hunting tips.

Is this a real store?

Not yet! Braden Tooth & Tide is a demo website. The address, phone number, reviews and prices are examples.
