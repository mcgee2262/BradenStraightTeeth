Our Story

From a Jar of Teeth to a Shop by the Sea

Braden Tooth & Tide started the way most collections do: one tiny black tooth spotted in the shell line, and a kid who couldn’t stop looking for the next one.

BD

Owner & CEO

Braden Dowling

Braden grew up walking the beaches of Oak Island at low tide, eyes locked on the shell line. Every sand tiger, every bull shark tooth, and eventually that first beach-worn megalodon went into a jar, then a shoebox, then a display case.

Today he runs Braden Tooth & Tide with a simple promise: real fossils, honest grades, and a story behind every tooth. Ask him about any tooth in the shop and he’ll tell you where it came from, how old it is, and how to find one yourself.
